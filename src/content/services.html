Our Medical Services

Comprehensive healthcare services designed to meet all your medical needs under one roof with our team of specialized physicians.

General Medicine

Comprehensive primary care for adults including preventive screenings, chronic disease management, routine check-ups, and acute illness treatment.

  • Annual Physical Exams
  • Chronic Disease Management
  • Preventive Care
  • Vaccinations

Pediatric Care

Specialized healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents including well-child visits, immunizations, and developmental assessments.

  • Well-Child Visits
  • Immunizations
  • Growth Monitoring
  • School Physicals

Cardiology

Expert cardiac care including EKG testing, stress tests, echocardiograms, and comprehensive management of heart conditions.

  • EKG Testing
  • Stress Tests
  • Heart Disease Management
  • Blood Pressure Monitoring

Laboratory Services

On-site lab testing for blood work, urinalysis, and diagnostic screenings with quick and accurate results.

  • Blood Tests
  • Urinalysis
  • Cholesterol Screening
  • Diabetes Testing

Neurology

Diagnosis and treatment of disorders affecting the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system including headaches and neuropathy.

  • Migraine Treatment
  • Nerve Disorders
  • Memory Evaluation
  • Sleep Studies

Orthopedics

Treatment for bone, joint, and muscle conditions including sports injuries, arthritis care, and rehabilitation.

  • Joint Pain Treatment
  • Sports Injuries
  • Arthritis Care
  • Physical Therapy

Ophthalmology

Complete eye care services including vision exams, glaucoma screening, and treatment of various eye conditions.

  • Vision Exams
  • Glaucoma Screening
  • Cataract Evaluation
  • Eye Disease Treatment

Dermatology

Skin care services including acne treatment, skin cancer screening, eczema management, and cosmetic procedures.

  • Skin Cancer Screening
  • Acne Treatment
  • Eczema Care
  • Mole Removal

Physical Therapy

Rehabilitation services to help you recover from injuries, surgeries, or chronic conditions through targeted exercises.

  • Post-Surgery Rehab
  • Pain Management
  • Mobility Training
  • Strength Building

Urgent Care

Walk-in services for non-life-threatening illnesses and injuries when you need care fast without an appointment.

  • Same-Day Visits
  • Minor Injuries
  • Cold & Flu Treatment
  • Infections

Vaccinations

Complete immunization services for all ages including flu shots, travel vaccines, and childhood immunizations.

  • Flu Shots
  • Travel Vaccines
  • Childhood Immunizations
  • COVID-19 Vaccines

Imaging Services

State-of-the-art diagnostic imaging including X-rays, ultrasounds, and referrals for advanced imaging.

  • X-Rays
  • Ultrasounds
  • Bone Density Scans
  • Imaging Referrals
